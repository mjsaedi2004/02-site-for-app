# NetRate brand files

The owner's logo (sent Nov 2026), kept here for every NetRate site and asset.

| File | What it is | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `netrate-icon-gradient.webp` | The app icon as sent: the white NR mark on the blue gradient, 1033 × 1033, square corners | The source of all icon sizes |
| `netrate-icon-gradient-1024.png` … `-32.png` | PNG sizes of the icon (180 = Apple touch icon, 64 / 32 = favicons) | Store-style tiles, favicons, link previews |
| `netrate-icon-rounded-512.png` | The icon with an app-icon corner, transparent outside | Places that need the rounded tile as an image |
| `netrate-mark-white.svg` | The mark alone, white vector on transparent (a trace: best at small and medium sizes) | Headers, footers, buttons on dark or blue grounds |
| `netrate-mark-white.webp`, `-2000.png`, `-1024.png` | The mark alone, white on transparent, raster | Large uses where the traced vector would show steps |

Colours sampled from the icon's gradient (light from the top-left and bottom-right corners, near-black on the other
diagonal): navy `#000C5C`, deep blue `#001388`, blue `#0129F4`, bright blue `#034AFD`, near-black `#010224`.

Rules: use the files as they are (don't redraw or recolour the mark). On light backgrounds, use the gradient icon tile;
the white mark needs a dark or blue ground. The app demo (`site/app/`) still shows its own older mark on its About
screen; changing that is a change to the app itself.

The owner's poster `netrate-poster-uk.webp` ("NetRate.app", Nov 2026): the NetRate × Design 1 page uses its graphic part only (the phone with the NetRate icon among the other apps, `site/studies/01-fintech/img/home-screen.webp`); its "best exchange rates" line, currency coins, UK flag and Apple badge stay out because of the NetRate brief (see CLAUDE.md).
