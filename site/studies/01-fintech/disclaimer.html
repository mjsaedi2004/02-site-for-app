Information only

The offices post the rates themselves, for information only; for a firm rate, contact the office before you deal. NetRate is not an exchange office and never moves money.

Accuracy and age

Every rate shows how long ago it was posted. Rates can change after they are posted, and an office may give a different rate when you contact it. Expired rates are labelled and left out of the order and the average.

No advice and no predictions

NetRate does not predict rates and gives no financial, legal or tax advice. The chart shows past market averages only.

The offices

Each office is responsible for its own rates, fees, services and compliance. A “Verified” badge says how the office was checked; it is not a guarantee of its services.

On this website

The app screens, numbers, office names and addresses shown on this website are demo data; they are made up to show how NetRate works.
