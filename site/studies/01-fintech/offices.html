Post your rate every day from a simple panel

The NetRate web panel works well on a phone, and you get a reminder to post your rate. Your rate is shown with the time it was posted, next to the rest.

Post your rate, with reminders

Every day from your phone or computer, in seconds.

A rate card with your logo

A polished card from that rate, as an image or text, for your channel and social media.

Daily Featured spot

Limited and bought day by day, always clearly labelled “Featured”; its real rank is shown next to it.

Reports and stats

Weekly and monthly reports and full statistics.

How your rate is shown

  • Side by side with the other offices, with your fee included in the order for the amount a person types.
  • With the time it was posted. A rate not updated today is marked “Expired” for one day and left out of the order and the average.
  • No office gets a special frame or colour; a rank is just a number.

Becoming “Verified”

In one of two equal ways: two active verified offices vouch for you, or you give your company number and AML number and our team checks them. The badge cannot be bought and has only one level.

Reviews

Every review is checked before it is published, and you can see the reviews of your office on its page.
