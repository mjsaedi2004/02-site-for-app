FAQ

Questions people often ask

The same answers you'll find inside the app.

Is NetRate an exchange office?

No. NetRate is not an exchange office and never moves any money. It only puts the rates the offices themselves post side by side; the deal is directly between you and the office.

Is my money safe?

Your money never reaches us, so its safety depends on the office you deal with. The “Verified” badge, user ratings and “Always up to date” on each office's page are there for exactly that; look at them before you deal.

Why do offices' rates differ?

Each office has its own rate and fee, and its cash, bank transfer and official invoice rates differ too. That's why, once you type an amount, the order is by the money you get after the fee.

What does an expired rate mean?

A rate the office hasn't updated today. It stays for one day, faded and labelled, then goes; it's never counted in the order or the average.

How does an office become “Verified”?

In one of two equal ways: two active verified offices vouch for it, or it gives its company number and AML number and our team checks them. The badge can't be bought and has only one level.

Why are there so few rates on Sunday?

Most UK offices are closed on Sundays. That day you see last week's summary, and the offices that are open come to the top of the list.

Do I need an account to see rates?

No. Rates, the office list, the chart and search all work without an account. You only need one for rate alerts, saved offices and writing reviews.
