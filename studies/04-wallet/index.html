Ask a question
How do I create an account?

Download the app, tap Create wallet and write down your recovery phrase. That is all; no paperwork in this sample.

What cryptocurrencies are supported?

More than 160 sample tokens, from the largest networks to new ones, all in one wallet.

Are there any transaction fees?

Only the network fee, shown before you confirm. Stalwart adds nothing on top.

Is the app available worldwide?

Yes, in most countries; a few features depend on local rules.

Can I recover my wallet if I lose access?

Yes, with your recovery phrase you can restore your wallet on any device.

How fast are transactions?

Most transfers settle within seconds; busy networks can take a little longer.

Where can I get support?

In the app under Help, any time of day.
