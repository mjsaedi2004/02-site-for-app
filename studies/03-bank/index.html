We’ll find the answers to all your questions

Why should I choose Nula?

One card, one app and no queues: open an account in minutes, see every payment in real time and get support around the clock.

How do I transfer money to another bank?

You can transfer money to any bank instantly, without commission and limits, if you have a Nula gold card. To apply for a card right now, log in to the “Cards” section of the application and fill in the required fields to receive a card.

How do I apply for a debit card?

Open the app, go to “Cards”, choose a design and confirm your details. The card appears in the app at once; the plastic one arrives by post.

How do I apply for a credit card?

Credit cards are available after a short check in the app. Choose a limit, read the terms and sign with one tap.

How do I activate Nula Pro?

In the app, open Profile, then Plans, and switch on Pro. The first month is on us in this sample.
